The four payment marks in this directory were exported from the client's own
Figma file, node 19:3720 (Footer, Device=Mobile, Language=UA), and are served
from this repo for the same reason the fonts and the flags are: no visitor
request reaches a third party.

  pay-visa.svg        74 x 24
  pay-mastercard.svg  38.769 x 24
  pay-tether.svg      24 x 24
  pay-bitcoin.svg     24 x 24

The sizes are the design's own and are set explicitly in css/shell.css rather
than being left to a single shared rule: the four marks have three different
aspect ratios, and one rule that fits the Visa wordmark stretches the round
ones.

Visa, Mastercard, Tether and Bitcoin are trademarks of their respective owners.
They appear here only to state which payment methods the operator accepts,
which is the use the design puts them to. They are not covered by this repo's
licence, and none of them is Top Win's mark.
